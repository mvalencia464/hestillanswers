## Uncertain sections

- Wonders rows that continue into the next heading are cut at the incident: the dark-lane boy, the Madison Avenue gift, the 1877 bandbox, the New Haven check.
- The New Haven sentence says "my children," and the card does not change it to another reading of Matthew 25.
- His Covenant is a string of promises with no story. Three verse cards take three sentences from it and do not invent an incident. The book does not print the chapter numbers.
- Come unto me and I am the Way are Cyclopaedia illustrations. The entries do not give a named incident after the sentence.
- Cyclopaedia scans are uneven. Captions keep the names, places, and spoken lines that read clearly, and they say where a letter is broken. They do not repair a broken word into a new fact.
- Wolfe at Quebec and the two cups are deaths, not cures. Sabat's sentence is a return from faith, not a healing.
- The two boys and the top ends in a killing. It is in the book. It is not a medical story.
- Skipped: medical cures and healings, drink and tobacco deliverances, Dr. Cullis and the consumptive home, Melancthon's illness, the infidel's decline, and fables (the king kissed by the devil, Daedalus, the Golden Age).
- Samuel Hick's rain and Lincoln's faith were not reused.
