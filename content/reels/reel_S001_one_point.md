# Reel: "One Point" (S001)

**Source:** *The Wonders of Prayer* (1885), "Changing the Course One Point", as told by a correspondent of the *Watchman*. Public domain.
**Format:** Facebook Reel, 9:16 (1080×1920), about 1:52. Scenes carry **no text**. British male narrator (ElevenLabs voice locked in STATUS.md).
**Look:** match the S001 feed image: brass binnacle, small oil lamp, dark deck, blurred captain in a greatcoat and peaked cap.

## What the story says, and nothing more

Kept exactly as recorded: Rio de Janeiro hotel after a walk across Palace Square · a very large gold medal from the British government · for saving a ship's company at sea · one night at sea, just before turning in, he gave the officer of the watch the course · while undressing, the impression to change course one point · no reason: the ship was on the right course for her port · tried to sleep, only four hours to his watch · could not throw it off · went on deck, gave the order · asleep as soon as his head was on the pillow · next day sighted a ship in distress, made sail for her · sinking · rescued the whole ship's company · shortly after, a gale destroyed the ship · had he not changed course, all would have perished · the writer's question, quoted word for word.

**Not in the account, so not in the Reel:** names, ship names, flags or nationality of the sinking ship, the year, the weather that night, the number of people saved, any prayer by the captain. Visuals stay general (night sea, a sinking sailing ship, boats) and never add a fact.

## Continuity (use in every prompt)

- **Captain:** American ship-master, about 45, short dark beard, dark wool greatcoat, peaked cap. Always partly turned away or soft-focus, never a sharp face portrait.
- **His ship:** wooden three-masted merchant sailing ship, worn deck planks, brass binnacle with a small oil lamp, wooden wheel.
- **Style lock (append to every prompt):** *Photorealistic, 1850s–1860s, 50mm f/1.8, shallow depth of field, sharp foreground subject, warm dim single light source, muted earth tones, 9:16 vertical. Keep the main subject in the middle of the frame; keep the bottom fifth simple (Reels buttons and captions sit there). No text, no lettering, no watermark, no modern objects, no electric light, no CGI look.*
